Objectives

Every daily quest, weekly quest and achievement is one row here. They differ by kind, which is a statement about how often the counter resets and nothing else. What an objective pays is not on this page — that is a reward rule on OBJECTIVE_COMPLETED with the objective key as its reference, so currency has one place it can come from.

Retire, don’t delete. Clearing Active stops an objective being offered and stops it counting, and loses nothing — a completed objective is somebody’s record and the ledger has to stay explicable. Delete is there for the objective authored by mistake: it destroys every player counter against it, so it is refused with a breakdown of what would go until you confirm a second time, and the reward rule that pays for the key survives as a rule nothing can trigger. Key, kind, metric, scope, aggregation and the game/grade/language filters are frozen once saved: every progress row already counting is counting under the old meaning. Retune the target, window, art and ordering instead, or author a new objective.
