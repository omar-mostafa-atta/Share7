Offers
Offers cannot be edited — there is no update endpoint, so an offer is authored once. purchases counts completed buys across all accounts, and any transaction at all (a refused attempt included) makes an offer undeletable as well, at which point only its expiry date can still take it off sale. A purchase limit is per account; leave it blank for unlimited.
